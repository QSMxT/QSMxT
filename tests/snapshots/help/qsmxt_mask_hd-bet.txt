Deep-learning brain extraction (HD-BET; downloads weights on first use)

Usage: qsmxt mask hd-bet [OPTIONS] --output <OUTPUT> <INPUT>

Arguments:
  <INPUT>
          Input NIfTI file (an image for the generating subcommands, a mask for the rest)

Options:
  -o, --output <OUTPUT>
          Output mask NIfTI file

      --op <OPS>
          Further refinement, applied in order after the subcommand's own operation (repeatable). Examples: erode:2, dilate:1, fill-holes:0, close:1, gaussian:4.0, majority (3x3x3 majority filter), largest-component, imfill (every hole), min-intensity:100 (remove voxels below 100 in the input image; needs an image input), signal-erode (signal-gated erosion of low-signal boundary voxels; needs a magnitude input)

      --window
          Run in 192x192x96 sliding-window patches (about 3.6–4.5 GB measured) instead of on the whole volume (the default; about 6–8 GB measured, and about twice as fast)

      --low-memory
          Use 128x128x64 sliding-window patches (about 1.5–1.9 GB measured)

      --patch <PATCH>
          Sliding-window patch size XxYxZ in voxels at 1 mm (multiples of 32x32x16)

      --tta
          8-fold mirroring test-time augmentation (about 8x slower)

      --tile-step <TILE_STEP>
          Sliding-window step as a fraction of the patch, in (0, 1]. Larger steps mean fewer patches and a shorter run: 0.5 (default) is HD-BET's own, 1.0 abuts the patches. Implies --window

  -h, --help
          Print help
