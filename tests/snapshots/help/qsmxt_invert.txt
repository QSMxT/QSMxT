Dipole inversion

Usage: qsmxt invert <COMMAND>

Commands:
  rts          Rapid Two-Step (RTS)
  tv           Total Variation (ADMM)
  tkd          Truncated K-space Division
  tsvd         Truncated Singular Value Decomposition
  tgv          Total Generalized Variation
  tikhonov     Tikhonov regularization
  nltv         Nonlinear Total Variation
  medi         Morphology Enabled Dipole Inversion
  tfi          Total Field Inversion (preconditioned). Input is the TOTAL field (not a local field)
  ilsqr        Iterative Least Squares QR
  star         STAR-QSM (Streaking Artifact Reduction): sparse inversion from a k-space initial guess
  lsqr         Minimally regularised LSQR
  heidi        LSQR+HEIDI (Homogeneity Enabled Incremental Dipole Inversion), or HEIDI on another inversion's map (`--seed`)
  ndi          Nonlinear Dipole Inversion
  fansi        FANSI Nonlinear Total Variation
  fansi-tgv    FANSI Nonlinear Total Generalized Variation
  l1qsm        L1 Data-Fidelity QSM
  whqsm        Weak-Harmonic QSM
  hdqsm        Hybrid Data-Fidelity QSM
  amp-pe       AMP-PE (Approximate Message Passing with Parameter Estimation)
  xqsm         xQSM deep-learning dipole inversion (weights downloaded on first use)
  qsmnet       QSMnet deep-learning dipole inversion (weights downloaded on first use)
  qsmnet-plus  QSMnet+ deep-learning dipole inversion (weights downloaded on first use)
  qsmnetme     QSMnetME deep-learning dipole inversion, the χ-separation toolbox's QSM network (weights downloaded on first use)
  autoqsm      AutoQSM single-step reconstruction — input is the TOTAL field (weights downloaded on first use)
  qsmgan       QSMGAN deep-learning dipole inversion (weights downloaded on first use)
  ir2qsm       IR2QSM deep-learning dipole inversion (weights downloaded on first use)
  lpcnn        LPCNN deep-learning dipole inversion (weights downloaded on first use)
  modl-qsm     MoDL-QSM deep-learning dipole inversion → χ33/STI component (weights downloaded on first use)
  nextqsm      NeXtQSM single-step reconstruction — input is the TOTAL field (weights downloaded on first use)
  iqsm         iQSM single-step reconstruction — input is every echo's WRAPPED phase (weights downloaded on first use)
  iqsm-plus    iQSM+ orientation-adaptive single-step reconstruction — input is every echo's WRAPPED phase; echoes are combined with (magnitude × TE)² weights (weights downloaded on first use)
  cosmos       COSMOS — multi-orientation least squares over 2+ co-registered orientations
  sti          STI (susceptibility tensor imaging) — 6+ co-registered orientations to a rank-2 tensor
  help         Print this message or the help of the given subcommand(s)

Options:
  -h, --help
          Print help
