Morphology Enabled Dipole Inversion

Usage: qsmxt invert medi [OPTIONS] --mask <MASK> --output <OUTPUT> --field-strength <FIELD_STRENGTH> --echo-times <ECHO_TIMES>... --magnitude <MAGNITUDE>... <INPUT>

Arguments:
  <INPUT>
          Input local field NIfTI file

Options:
  -m, --mask <MASK>
          Binary mask NIfTI file

  -o, --output <OUTPUT>
          Output susceptibility map NIfTI file

      --b0-direction <B0_DIRECTION> <B0_DIRECTION> <B0_DIRECTION>
          B0 direction (3 values)

          [default: 0 0 1]

      --tile-size <TILE_SIZE>
          DL tiling: output core size per patch, in voxels (enables tiling)

      --tile-halo <TILE_HALO>
          DL tiling: context margin per side, in voxels (default 8)

      --field-strength <FIELD_STRENGTH>
          B0 field strength in Tesla

      --echo-times <ECHO_TIMES>...
          Echo times in seconds, every echo's, in order

      --magnitude <MAGNITUDE>...
          Magnitude of every echo: one 4D NIfTI, or one 3D NIfTI per echo in echo order

      --no-medi-smv
          MEDI: no SMV high-pass of the local field

      --medi-smv
          MEDI: high-pass the local field with a spherical mean value filter, removing residual background near the brain edge (MEDI-SMV; on by default)

      --medi-smv-radius <MEDI_SMV_RADIUS>
          MEDI SMV radius in mm (also mSMV's) (default: 5)

      --no-medi-smv-shrink-mask
          MEDI-SMV: keep the full mask, high-passing the field over it

      --medi-smv-shrink-mask
          MEDI-SMV: shrink the mask to where the SMV of the mask exceeds 0.999 (on by default)

      --medi-msmv
          MEDI-mSMV (Roberts et al. 2024): filter the local field by maximum spherical mean value before MEDI, which fits it with the SMV-filtered dipole kernel (it implies the SMV). Needs at least 3 echoes

      --medi-msmv-maxk <MEDI_MSMV_MAXK>
          MEDI-mSMV: the boundary correction makes at most this many passes, less one (default: 5)

      --medi-msmv-vessel-radius <MEDI_MSMV_VESSEL_RADIUS>
          MEDI-mSMV: largest vessel thickness of the vessel mask, in voxels (default: 15, or 8 × the largest voxel dimension if that is more)

      --no-medi-msmv-prefilter
          MEDI-mSMV: no SMV prefilter before mSMV. Only consistent for a field already high-passed by the same SMV; after PDF, V-SHARP or LBV it diverges (expert)

      --medi-lambda <MEDI_LAMBDA>
          MEDI lambda: the data-fidelity weight (larger fits the field more closely) (default: 1000)

      --medi-percentage <MEDI_PERCENTAGE>
          MEDI edge percentage (0.0-1.0) (default: 0.9)

      --medi-max-iter <MEDI_MAX_ITER>
          MEDI: Maximum outer iterations (default: 10)

      --medi-cg-max-iter <MEDI_CG_MAX_ITER>
          MEDI: Maximum conjugate gradient iterations (default: 100)

      --medi-cg-tol <MEDI_CG_TOL>
          MEDI: CG convergence tolerance (default: 0.01)

      --medi-tol <MEDI_TOL>
          MEDI: Outer convergence tolerance (default: 0.1)

      --medi-merit <MEDI_MERIT>
          MEDI: enable MERIT weighting (default: true)

          [possible values: true, false]

      --medi-csf <MEDI_CSF>
          MEDI+0 (Liu et al. 2018): zero-reference χ to the ventricular CSF, found from R2*, and keep it uniform there (default: true). Needs at least 3 echoes; with fewer, MEDI runs without it

          [possible values: true, false]

      --medi-lambda-csf <MEDI_LAMBDA_CSF>
          MEDI+0: weight of the CSF uniformity term (default: 100)

      --medi-csf-r2s-threshold <MEDI_CSF_R2S_THRESHOLD>
          MEDI+0: R2* in Hz below which a voxel is a CSF candidate (default: 5)

      --medi-csf-erode <MEDI_CSF_ERODE>
          MEDI+0: erode the mask by a 10 mm sphere before finding the CSF (default: true)

          [possible values: true, false]

      --medi-zeropad <X> <Y> <Z>
          MEDI: voxels of zeros appended at the end of x, y and z before the inversion; the result is cropped back (default: 0 0 0)

      --medi-data-weighting <MEDI_DATA_WEIGHTING>
          MEDI: data weighting mode (0=uniform, 1=SNR) (default: 1)

      --medi-te <TE>
          MEDI: the echo time the field is converted to phase at: first, last, spacing (TE2 - TE1), sum (of the echo times), or a time in ms (default: spacing)

  -h, --help
          Print help
