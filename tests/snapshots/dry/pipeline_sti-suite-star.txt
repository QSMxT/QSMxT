# Methods

QSM processing was performed using qsmxt (Stewart, 2026). Processing followed the STI Suite 3.0 pipeline. Inhomogeneity correction (Eckstein et al., 2019) was applied to each echo's magnitude. A brain mask was generated using Otsu thresholding (Otsu, 1979) of the ROMEO phase quality map (computed from phase data and the inhomogeneity-corrected RSS-combined magnitude), followed by dilation, hole-filling, and erosion. Phase was converted to ppm with the scanner's centre frequency (ImagingFrequency). Phase unwrapping was performed separately for each echo, on the raw phase over the whole volume, using the Laplacian method (Schofield & Zhu, 2003) with an FFT Poisson solve on the volume zero-padded by 12 voxels. The B0 field map was estimated as a weighted average of the unwrapped echo phases with tes weighting. After background field removal, the mask was reduced to its largest connected component with its holes filled, and the local field was set to zero outside it. Background field removal was performed using V-SHARP (Wu et al., 2012). The V-SHARP background was estimated on an isotropic grid: the field and mask were cropped to the brain mask's bounding box and resampled trilinearly, and the background field estimated there was interpolated back to the acquired grid, so the local field kept the acquired resolution. Dipole inversion was performed using STAR-QSM (Streaking Artifact Reduction) (Wei et al., 2015) with zero-padding of 8 × 8 × 20 voxels per side and the field converted to phase at the sum of the echo times. No susceptibility referencing was applied.
