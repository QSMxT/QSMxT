# Methods

QSM processing was performed using qsmxt (Stewart, 2026). Processing followed the STI Suite 3.0 pipeline. A brain mask was generated using thresholding of the last-echo magnitude image at STI Suite's default level (Otsu's threshold of the middle slice; Otsu, 1979), followed by the largest connected component kept and every hole filled. Phase was converted to ppm with the scanner's centre frequency (ImagingFrequency). Phase unwrapping was performed separately for each echo, on the raw phase over the whole volume, using the Laplacian method (Schofield & Zhu, 2003) with an FFT Poisson solve on the volume zero-padded by 12 voxels. The B0 field map was estimated as a weighted average of the unwrapped echo phases with tes weighting. After background field removal, the mask was reduced to its largest connected component with its holes filled, and the local field was set to zero outside it. Background field removal was performed using V-SHARP (Wu et al., 2012). The V-SHARP background was estimated on an isotropic grid: the field and mask were cropped to the brain mask's bounding box and resampled trilinearly, and the background field estimated there was interpolated back to the acquired grid, so the local field kept the acquired resolution. Dipole inversion was performed using iLSQR (Li et al., 2015) with at most 40 iterations and precision = single. No susceptibility referencing was applied.
